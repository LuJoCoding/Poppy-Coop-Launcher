POPPY PLAYTIME CO-OP - LAUNCHER 1.4.8 (Chapter 1 + 2)
=====================================================

Unofficial PoppyCoop Launcher 3.0 (fan mod, not by Mob Entertainment).
New compared to the original:
- New launcher: you no longer have to set up Chapter 1 to play Chapter 2 - the setup (game folder,
  installing the mod) only comes when you click a chapter.
- Settings per chapter ("Settings" button): keys (teleport, buddy figure, record, replay) - click and
  press the new key; switches for what is shared (doors/puzzles, items, inventory, hands, pullable
  objects, loaded areas, minigames, messages); reinstall, remove the mod, change the folder.
- The launcher finds the game by itself - also a version downpatched with the Steam console (it always lands in
  Steam\steamapps\content\app_...\depot_...). If it finds nothing, it says so and you pick the folder.
- "Launcher settings" (bottom left): theme (Dark / Midnight / Light), accent colour, animations.
- Server card (bottom left): pick the server and see how good the connection is (signal bars, ping, jitter,
  loss). Besides the free PoppyCoop relay you can host on your own PC or add a friend's IP / another relay.
  Everyone you play with has to pick the same server.
- One exe for Windows and Linux (Steam/Proton). Name, colour, codes and game folders from the old
  launcher are kept.
- Chapter 2 is playable in the launcher (beta).
- Real player figures in Chapter 2: full-body figure with GrabPack (backpack, launchers, hands),
  animations for standing, walking, running, crouching, jumping; launched hands fly with a black
  cable to where your friend actually shoots; the green hand is shown green on the figure.
- New player figure in Chapter 1: a faceless mannequin with a round head in the player's colour, with
  joints (walking, running, crouching, jumping, lying down when dead) and the real Chapter 1 GrabPack
  (backpack, both launchers, hands on the tubes). GrabPack and red hand appear once the player has them.
- Chapter 1: launched hands are the real game hands - same angle as your friend's, and when they hold
  on to something the hand on the figure is closed too. Short shots are shown as well.
- Chapter 1: boxes, toy boxes, blocks, chairs, tables, bins etc. are shared while moved and stay where
  they were left after a reload; the pull platforms in the factory move for your friend too (no more
  standing in mid-air).
- Game freezes in Chapter 1 fixed.
- Chapter 2: everything the GrabPack can pull (barrels, buckets, boxes, bins, ladders, toy blocks,
  toy boxes, chairs...) moves for your friend too and stays where it was left.
- Chapter 2 minigames: Musical Memory - whoever wins, wins for everyone. Whack-a-Wuggy - play it
  together: everyone's hits add up.
- Chapter 2: a crouching player's figure crouches on the floor instead of floating.
- The green hand is shared: if one player has it, everyone gets it.
- Doors, floor hatches, levers, crane handles, vents, spider webs, wooden walls, cut-outs, carts etc.
  move for your friend exactly as for you and stay that way after a reload.
- Puzzle pieces (furnace doors, gears, molds, paint cartridges, garage levers) are shared.
- Power, switches, keys, items and checkpoints are shared
  ("Load Game" brings everyone to the furthest checkpoint).
- For 2, 3 or more players: everyone joins the same room.
- F6: teleport to another player (with several players each press goes to the next one). Chapter 2: if
  that player is at a later checkpoint, you first respawn at their checkpoint (short loading screen), so
  chases/games they already finished are finished for you too. If a friend escapes a chase you are still
  in, you are brought to them automatically.
  Their area is loaded first ("Loading ...'s area"), so you no longer fall out of the map.
  Keys can be changed in the launcher (chapter "Settings"; F8/F9 stay free for other mods).
- Crash when reloading a checkpoint fixed.
- The launcher runs much more smoothly.

Installation (Windows)
----------------------
1. Unzip, start PoppyCoopLauncher.exe.
   Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" (the exe is not signed):
   "More info" -> "Run anyway".
2. Click the chapter you want to play. Only then the launcher asks for its game folder (usually it
   finds it itself, otherwise "Browse": Steam -> right-click the game -> Manage -> Browse local
   files), your name and colour -> "Install co-op mod".
3. If you used an older version: just start this launcher and press PLAY, the mod is updated to
   1.4.8 automatically. Above PLAY it should then say "Co-op mod v1.4.8 installed". Otherwise click
   "Reinstall" once in "Settings".
4. Keys and what is shared: "Settings" at the top right of the chapter. Everyone in the room should
   use the same switches.

Playing together
----------------
- One player presses "Create Game" and shares the room code, everyone else presses "Join Game" and enters it.
- Everyone loads the SAME checkpoint in the game (Play -> Load Game).
- Everyone needs this launcher version (1.4.8), otherwise the mods don't match.
- Firewall prompt for PoppyCoopNet.exe: "Allow".
- F6 = teleport to another player (cycles), T = ping the spot you look at, F10 = status (fps, ping, where your friends are), Y = quick chat, F7 = buddy figure, O/P = record/replay your movement.
- Checkpoints only move forward: a player who is behind doesn't reset the others.
- Only share the room code with people you want to play with.
- In game, press F10 and type "coop_status" to see the room and players.
